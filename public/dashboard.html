Add Custom Domain

Connect your own domain to your YENZE project.

Enter your domain without http:// or https://

📝 DNS Configuration

After adding your domain, configure these DNS records with your domain provider (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.):

For root domain (@):
Type: A
Name: @ (or leave empty)
Value: 76.76.21.21
TTL: 600 (or Auto)
For www subdomain:
Type: CNAME
Name: www
Value: cname.vercel-dns.com
TTL: 600 (or Auto)

⏱️ DNS propagation can take 5-30 minutes. Once configured, click "Verify Domain" in your dashboard.
