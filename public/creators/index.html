Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid?

We use Stripe Connect for instant payouts. When someone buys your template, 70% goes directly to your connected bank account. Stripe handles all the payment processing securely.

What's the revenue split?

You keep 70% of every sale. YENZE takes 30% to cover payment processing, hosting, marketing, and platform maintenance. This is one of the highest creator payouts in the industry.

What kind of templates can I sell?

HTML templates of all kinds: landing pages, portfolios, dashboards, linktree alternatives, business sites, and more. Templates should be well-designed, responsive, and use clean code.

How do I submit templates?

After setting up your Stripe account, email your template files to hello@yenze.io. Include a preview image and suggested price. We review all submissions for quality before publishing.

What prices can I set?

You can price your templates between $10 and $99. We recommend starting at $15-29 for single-page templates and $39-49 for multi-page or complex designs.

Are there any upfront costs?

No! It's completely free to join. You only pay when you make money (through the 30% platform fee). There are no monthly fees, listing fees, or hidden costs.
